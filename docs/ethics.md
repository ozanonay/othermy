# How OtherMy handles the hard parts

OtherMy sits close to memory, family and grief. These are the rules we build by, and how each one
shows up in the app.

## A copy is not the person

- A copy answers from what it was told: answers, memories, recordings, real chats. It doesn't think,
  feel or continue anyone's life, and it can't know what the person would make of something new.
- When it doesn't know, it says so, in the person's own way, instead of inventing a fact.
- If someone asks whether they're talking to the real person, the copy says honestly and briefly that
  it is a digital copy made from that person's words.
- Before the first conversation with a copy of someone who has passed away, the app explains this
  gently, and you can choose "Not Today".

## Consent, especially for voices

- Your own copy is yours: you decide what goes in, and you can change or delete any of it.
- A copy of someone else is made by people who loved them. Only that person's own messages teach it
  how they talk; your side of a chat is context, never training.
- A voice is made only from the person's own recordings, only with their consent or another legal
  right, and it can be removed at any time. Every call is labelled as a conversation with an AI copy.
- A child's words in *For My Child* never teach a copy. Children can't consent to that, so we don't.

## Your data

- What you share never trains public AI models. No ads; data is never sold.
- One file with everything (answers, chats, memories, family tree, photos, recordings), any time.
- Delete any part, or your whole account.
- If OtherMy ever closes, at least 90 days' notice, with downloads working until the last day, for
  you and for your guardians.
- OtherMy is not end-to-end encrypted. We'd rather say so than let anyone assume otherwise.

## After someone dies

- Guardians (up to five trusted people) can do nothing while you're here. If they report that you've
  passed away, you get 30 days and several reminders to say you're fine; only then do they look
  after your copy, as you set it up.
- Messages for the Future that are meant for "after I'm gone" go out only once your guardians
  confirm it, never on a timer alone.

## Grief

- OtherMy is not therapy and doesn't claim to treat anything. If someone seems to be in danger or
  crisis, the copy gently encourages them to reach out to people around them or local emergency
  services.
- We avoid design that pushes people to come back: no streaks, no guilt. A copy may write first
  now and then, the way a person would, and that can be turned off.
- We don't use words like "immortality" or "bring them back". The app keeps words and stories; it
  doesn't promise a person.
